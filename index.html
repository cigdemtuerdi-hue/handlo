How Handlo works

01

List it

Post a used item with a starting bid, optional buyout, and 12, 24, or 48 hours on the clock.

02

Swap or sell

Someone bids up or hits buyout. The piece leaves your house and stays in use.

03

Cut the footprint

Each sale shows estimated CO₂e avoided versus making that item new. A loop, not a landfill.

Recycling is the product

Handlo is not a donation platform. It is a market. The green part is reuse: one less new mixer, bike, chair, or camera.

  • Estimated carbon avoided is printed on every auction.
  • Your Impact tab totals what you kept in circulation.
  • USA only. Independent reuse marketplace.
